# TypeTussle 🎮

**TypeTussle** is an interactive word battle game where you compete against a robot opponent. Guess letters to reveal the hidden word and drain your opponent’s energy before your own runs out. Built entirely with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

---

## 🕹 How to Play

1. A word is randomly selected and hidden.
2. A hint is displayed to help you guess the word.
3. Click letters on the keyboard to guess.
   - Correct guesses reduce the robot’s energy.
   - Wrong guesses reduce your energy.
4. The game ends when either your energy or the robot’s energy reaches zero.
5. Press **Restart** to play again with a new word.

---

## ✨ Features

- Dynamic keyboard with clickable letters.
- Animated energy bars for both human and robot.
- Visual feedback with images for wins and losses.
- Sound effects for victory and defeat.
- Random word + hint system for replayability.

---

## 💻 Tech Stack

- **HTML5** – Structure of the game
- **CSS3** – Styling and animations
- **JavaScript (Vanilla)** – Game logic, DOM manipulation, event handling
